About the Union

A stronger voice begins with unity.

Representing workers. Supporting families. Building communities.

Indian Workers Union is a collective platform dedicated to representing workers and promoting dignity, fairness and welfare in the workplace.

Through member participation, awareness programmes, workplace engagement and welfare initiatives, the Union works to create a stronger and more informed community of workers.
